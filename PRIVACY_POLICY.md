# Privacy Policy for Jumper Game

**Last Updated: October 6, 2025**

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Jumper Game ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information Collected Automatically

Our app does not directly collect personal information. However, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

**Google AdMob**
- Our app displays advertisements through Google AdMob
- AdMob may collect device information including:
  - Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  - IP address
  - Device type and operating system
  - General location information
  - Ad interaction data
- For more information about how Google uses data, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Information We Store Locally

The app stores the following information locally on your device:
- High score data
- Game progress and statistics

This information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

## How We Use Information

We do not collect or use your personal information directly. The data collected by third-party services (AdMob) is used to:
- Display personalized advertisements
- Measure ad performance
- Improve ad relevance

## Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except:
- **Google AdMob**: As described above, for serving advertisements
- **Legal Requirements**: If required by law, regulation, or legal process

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. The app is designed to be safe for all ages and does not contain age-restricted content.

## Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

**Google AdMob**
- Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Used for displaying advertisements

## Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is minimal risk to your information. Game data stored locally on your device is subject to your device's security measures.

## Your Rights and Choices

**Advertising ID**
- You can reset your advertising ID or opt-out of personalized ads through your device settings:
  - **Android**: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization

**Data Deletion**
- All locally stored game data can be deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data through your device settings
